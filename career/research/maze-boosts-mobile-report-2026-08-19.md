## Task results

> **Read the misclick numbers with this caveat (Kirill, 2026-08-19).** Both
> studies used a mobile prototype, and part of the audience opened them on
> desktop. There the mobile frame needs scrolling, and those participants
> visibly produced more rage-clicks. So a share of every misclick rate here is
> an artefact of the testing environment, not of the interface. Treat the rates
> as directional — the ranking between tasks holds, the absolute percentages
> overstate the problem. In the case, state the caveat next to the numbers
> rather than quoting 74% bare.
