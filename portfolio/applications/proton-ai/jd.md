## Role

**UI Product Designer**
Full-time. Cambridge, MA hybrid (3d/w in Harvard Square) OR EU Remote.
Salary: $80,000–$100,000 USD/year + bonus + equity.

## Responsibilities

- Own visual design and UX across four Proton products

- Daily hands-on use of Claude Code, Cursor, or equivalent
- Portfolio showing workflow redesigns from legacy systems
- Systems thinking for design scalability
- Real agile experience, customer-facing preference

**Big Plus:**
- Complex B2B, enterprise, or ERP software
- Agentic product patterns
- Early-stage SaaS startups
