Finom is tackling the hardest UX problem in B2B fintech: regulatory flows — credit checks, KYC, repayment terms — that have to be honest about risk without driving users away. That exact tension is what I spent two years resolving at FinchTrade AG, where I designed MarketGuard: an AML/KYT compliance platform built from scratch for crypto businesses navigating post-FTX regulation.

MarketGuard is a multi-tenant B2B product with separate roles for traders, compliance officers, and admins — each with its own portal and workflow. The core design problem was identical to what the Credit & Cards domain will throw at me: translate a hard compliance requirement (in our case, a full AML case review process) into a flow users can complete without errors, without confusion, and without abandoning midway. We measured activation time and reduced it by 79% from v1. We also ran A/B tests and qualitative sessions at each major UX decision; the research artifacts are live in the portfolio at in-buro.com/works/marketguard. The parent product, FinchTrade, saw bounce rate drop from 68% to 14% and time-on-site go from 57 seconds to over three minutes in the months following the redesign — driven by the same research-then-test approach.

I have also spent the past year and a half as the solo designer at Beefy.finance, a DeFi yield optimizer with 12,000 monthly active users. I built the token-based design system, ran two rounds of unmoderated usability testing with 98 sessions total, and shipped the core UX — search, filtering, wallet connection, and the ZAP one-step deposit flow — through A/B testing and data. Working with a DAO-structured team spread across time zones has been useful training for Finom's async portfolio review and distributed model.
