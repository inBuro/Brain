# Case language — in-buro.com

One voice across every case, so two cases opened in a row read as one author.
Applies to `works/*/index.html`: headings, captions, table cells, tooltips.

## Voice

- **First person singular.** «I set the target», «I built the template system».
  Never «we» — the CV says sole designer, and «we» quietly hands the credit away.
  Exception: describing what the team did, and only when the split matters
  («the code was the front-end developer's»).
- **The reader is a hiring manager, not a client.** No pitching, no adjectives
  that cannot be checked. «Clear, confident, credible» is a claim; «loud reads as
  risky in this market» is a reason.
- **Every claim carries its source or its number.** If neither exists, say so
  plainly («not measured separately») rather than implying one.
- **Findings before decisions, decisions before results.** The Insight block is
  the shape of the whole case: what research found → what changed in the product
  → what it produced.
- **Failed iterations stay in.** v.2 raising CTR without moving time is the most
  convincing paragraph in the case; do not tidy it away.

## Spelling and punctuation

- **British spelling.** organised, optimisation, minimising, prioritise,
  behaviour. Not organized/optimization.
- **En dash `–` with spaces** for the parenthetical break. Never `—`, never `-`.
- **Oxford comma: yes.** «execution, reporting, and the tools they hand on».
  Both cases already lean this way; keep it consistent.
- **No trailing period in captions and table cells.** Full sentences in body
  text end normally.
- **Sentence case in headings.** «Onboarding, three versions deep», not Title Case.
- **Straight arrows `→` for before/after**: `324 → 68 minutes`, `32 → 89%`.
  Not «from 324 to 68».
- **Percentages tight to the number**: `89%`. Units spelled out after values:
  `68 minutes`, `48-hour delay`.

## Naming

- **Product names as the product writes them**: FinchTrade, MarketGuard,
  Spot / Market / RFQ trading, Settlements, Settings, Agent profile.
- **Roles capitalised** when named as roles: Trader, Compliance officer, Founder,
  Manager, Product manager. Lowercase when describing a person generically («the
  manager reviewing the file»).
- **The product role is always «Product manager»**, never bare «product» —
  «product evaluates it once» reads as the product evaluating itself.
- **Version labels**: `v.1`, `v.2`, `v.3` in captions and charts; «Version 1» in
  running prose only if the sentence opens with it.
- **Say what a thing is, not what it is like.** «A checklist holding every
  outstanding request», not «a smart onboarding solution».

## Structure of a case

1. Hero: title, one-paragraph lede, facts (role, scope, domain, period, team),
   up to three headline metrics, reference quote.
2. Context — who buys and how they decide.
3. Role and scope — what was mine, and the research method in one paragraph.
4. Insights — two to four, each in the three-row form.
5. Product sections — screens with captions that state the decision, not the UI.
6. Website / design system / communications — only what the case can prove.
7. Research — artefacts as data (tables), not screenshots of boards.

## Metrics

- Name the measure and the period: «fifth month of work against the month before».
- Never let a marketing metric masquerade as a product one: website numbers live
  in the website section, product numbers in the hero.
- If a number appears in the CV or Notion, it must match the case exactly —
  including the base it is calculated from.

## Before publishing

- No editorial tooltips (`note-body`) left in the page.
- No placeholder copy (`class="dim"`).
- `noindex` removed, canonical URL correct, links from CV/Notion pointing at it.
- Figma and the case agree on every number shown in both.
